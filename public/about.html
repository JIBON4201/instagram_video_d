About Instagram Reel Downloader

Instagram Reel Downloader is a free, self-hosted tool that lets you download Instagram videos, reels, photos, IGTV, stories, and profile pictures in high quality — including HD and 4K where available. No login, no watermark, and no database. Everything you download stays private to you.

Our Mission

We built Instagram Reel Downloader to give people a simple, fast, and private way to save public Instagram content they own or have permission to use. We believe downloading should be straightforward — no accounts, no watermarks, no hidden fees.

How It Works

Paste any Instagram link (reel, post, IGTV, story, or profile), pick a quality, and download. Our server uses yt-dlp to extract the best available stream and FFmpeg to deliver a universally playable MP4. Temporary files are deleted automatically after 30 minutes.
