Reel Downloader

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Information We Collect

Reel Downloader does not collect personal data. We do not require accounts, do not track users, and do not store downloaded videos permanently.

Instagram URLs

When you submit an Instagram reel URL, it is sent to our server to fetch metadata and download the video. The URL is processed temporarily and is not stored in a database. Server logs may record the request path (without query parameters) for operational purposes, but these logs are rotated and not retained long-term.

Cookies and Local Storage

The frontend stores a list of recently downloaded reels in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server. You can clear it at any time using the "Clear" button in the Recent downloads section.

Third Parties

This tool uses yt-dlp and FFmpeg to retrieve media from Instagram. These tools run on the server where the app is hosted. No video data is sent to any third-party downloader service.

Data Retention

Downloaded video files are stored in a temporary directory and are automatically deleted after a configurable period (default: 30 minutes). Metadata cache entries expire after 10 minutes by default.

Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no account data to access, correct, or delete. To remove local recent-history data, click "Clear" in the UI or clear your browser's localStorage for this site.

Only download content you own or have permission to use. This tool is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.
